One week

A grocery list with no sweets added

Every amount on this page is for seven days, one week of groceries, not one day.

The lists do not add candy, soda, or dessert. A pound of sugar in the United States price table is $1.019, and it does not fit the $50 or $100 list, so it is left off. The price is not cut. This page is not medical advice and not a treatment.
