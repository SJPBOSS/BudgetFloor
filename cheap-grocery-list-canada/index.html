A cheap grocery list for Canada

A list with a price beside each food is easier to shop than a general tip. On Budget Floor you pick Canada, the United States, or Mexico, then an amount. This page stays with Canada.

Every amount includes cabbage, bananas, and vegetable oil. The oil line is 500 ml. Statistics Canada publishes vegetable oil as 3 litres at $9.82, and 500 ml is one sixth of that bottle, which is $1.64 to the cent. Higher amounts add lentils, a can of beans, frozen spinach, carrots, canned tomatoes, a dozen eggs, and the 675 g loaf of white bread at its published $3.63.

Prices are the Canada average from Statistics Canada table 18-10-0245-01 for August 2026. The same tool shows United States and Mexico prices if you switch the country.
