A cheap grocery list for Canada

A list with a price beside each food is easier to shop than a general tip. On Budget Floor you pick Canada, the United States, or Mexico, then an amount. This page stays with Canada.

Every amount includes vegetables and cooking oil. The $5 shop is lentils, carrots, and oil. Higher amounts add oats, eggs, beans, spinach, bread, peanut butter, rice, onion, and pasta. Dinner is a pot with a vegetable in it. It is not a plate of plain rice and a single egg.

Prices are estimated, October 2026. They were already stored in Budget Floor, and no store page is on file, so this page does not name a grocer. Bananas and cabbage are left off, because no store page could be read for them. The same foods, with United States prices, are on the tool if you switch the country.
