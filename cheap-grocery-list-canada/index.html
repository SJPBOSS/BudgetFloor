A cheap grocery list for Canada

When money is short, a list with prices on it beats a general tip. Budget Floor is a free page with one job: you pick a dollar amount, and it shows a small Canadian grocery list that adds up to that amount. The same page can switch to US prices. This guide stays with the Canadian list.

The amounts are $5, $10, $15, $20, $25, and $30. From $10 upward, each list keeps the food from the step below and adds a few items. The $5 list is separate. It is rice and dried lentils, not a smaller copy of the $10 basket.

Open the $10 Canadian list

The $5 list

Two lines. The rice is the No Name bag named in the tool. The note on that button says, "CRITICAL: Under $5 in 2026 is emergency territory. Seek aid." Rice and lentils can be a meal. They are not a target to aim at if another option exists.

The $10 list

This is the foundation shop. The instruction on the button is: "Oats for breakfast. Rice + 1 Egg for dinner. Beans for protein stability."

There are 6 eggs, and the dinner line uses one egg, so that is six dinners of rice and egg before the carton is empty. The beans are a single can. The tool does not say how many bowls come out of the 1 kg of oats or the 900 g of rice. That depends on the scoop, and it is not written down here.

What gets added after $10

In the tool, the higher buttons do not repeat every food. The $15 list shows a line called "Tier $10 Base" at $10.00, then the new items. That base is the four foods above, not another product. The same pattern continues: "Tier $15 Base" means the whole $15 shop, and so on.

$15

Peanut butter and white bread. The $15 note calls peanut butter the caloric floor: the jar that makes a thin plate more filling, not a treat you wait for. These two lines sum to $5.00. The full shop, including the $10 foods, is $15.00.

Which list to open

  • You are shopping for yourself and want the breakfast and dinner named: start with the $10 list, then read Grocery budget for one person.
  • You want seven mornings and seven evenings written out: Budget meal plan for a week, built on the $15 shop.
  • More than one person is eating: Grocery budget for a family. A $10 basket is written as one plate.
  • You are comparing caps such as under $5 or under $10: Cheap meals under $10.
