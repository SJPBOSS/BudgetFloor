A cheap grocery list for Mexico

This is a cheap grocery list for Mexico, in pesos. The amounts are $100, $200, $300, and $500 MXN. Every list has tortillas, beans, carrots, and bananas. From $200, the list adds a litre of vegetable oil, eggs, oats, and a quarter kilogram of onion.

Each price is a Profeco observation for Mexico City in October 2026, from Quién es Quién en los Precios. For each product, the figure is the upper median of the city observations, then the latest row at that price. That is a regular observed price, not the cheapest row. Two of a package is two times that observation. The quarter kilogram of onion is 0.25 times $67 per kilogram, which is $16.75. The litre of oil is $43, so it is not on the $100 list.
