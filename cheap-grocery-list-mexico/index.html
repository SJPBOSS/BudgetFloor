A cheap grocery list for Mexico

This is a cheap grocery list for Mexico, in pesos. The amounts are $100, $200, $300, and $500 MXN. Every list has a vegetable, fruit, and cooking oil, plus tortillas and beans. The larger lists add eggs, rice, tomato, onion, lentils, cabbage, oats, and frozen peas.

Each price was read on a Soriana search page on 22 October 2026. The link in the table goes to that search. Where the tile showed a sale price, that lower price is the one used, and the crossed-out price is named in the row. A points price was not used. Two of a product are written as two times the shelf price. The quarter kilogram of white onion is 0.25 times $68.90 per kilogram, which is $17.225, rounded to $17.23. Walmart México did not return a readable page, so those prices are not here. Nothing on this list is guessed.
