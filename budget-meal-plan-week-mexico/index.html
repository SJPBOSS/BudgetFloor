A budget meal plan for a week in Mexico

Every amount on this page is for seven days, one week of groceries, not one day.

This week uses the $200 MXN list. The foods add up to $194.65. Breakfast is eggs with tortilla, or oats and a banana. The later meal is beans with carrot, onion, and oil, eaten with tortillas.

Prices are Profeco observations for Mexico City in October 2026. The onion is a quarter kilogram at $67 per kilogram, which is $16.75. If you can spend $300, the tool adds tomato, lentils, rice, and canned peas, for $297.95. At $500 the shop is $498.85 and can be shared. A kilogram of cabbage is $31, and it fits the $500 list, not this one.

The seven days

The beans are one 900 g bag, the bananas are 1 kg, and the eggs are a carton of 12. Split them across the week. Oats take some mornings so the eggs last.
