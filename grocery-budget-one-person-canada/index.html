A grocery budget for one person in Canada

Every amount on this page is for seven days, one week of groceries, not one day.

One person can start with the Canadian $10 list, which adds up to $9.79, or the $20 list, which adds up to $19.81. Both include cabbage, bananas, and oil. The $20 shop adds beans, spinach, carrots, and canned tomatoes, so the pot has more than one vegetable.

Prices are the Canada average from Statistics Canada table 18-10-0245-01 for August 2026. If $10 is the whole week and it still is not enough, the directories at the bottom are real.

The $10 shop for one person

Lentils with a kilogram of cabbage and a little oil, and bananas in the morning. The foods add up to $9.79.

This is written as one plate. It is not a family pot. For that, see the family page.

If this shop is more than you have, use a directory. 211.ca connects you to local help, and Food Banks Canada covers the country. In Quebec, 211 Québec and the Banques alimentaires du Québec do that job. In the United States, Feeding America finds a food bank. In Mexico, Bancos de Alimentos de México and their map of food banks do the same.
