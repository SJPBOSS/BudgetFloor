A grocery list and halal

Every amount on this page is for seven days, one week of groceries, not one day.

Canada’s lists contain no meat. Mexico’s lists contain no pork. United States lists from $20 include whole chicken at the published per-pound average. Budget Floor does not certify halal slaughter, ingredients, or handling. That chicken line is not a halal certification.

In Quebec, 211 Québec and the Banques alimentaires du Québec do that job. In the United States, Feeding America finds a food bank. In Mexico, Bancos de Alimentos de México and their map of food banks do the same.
