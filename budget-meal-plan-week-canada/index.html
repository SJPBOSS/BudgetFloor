A budget meal plan for a week in Canada

This week uses the Canadian $20 list. The foods add up to $20.00. Breakfast is oats or peanut butter on bread. Dinner always has a vegetable, rice, and oil. An egg sits on the plate six nights, because the carton has six eggs. One night uses the beans and skips the egg.

Prices are estimated, October 2026. They were already in Budget Floor. No store page is on file. The $15 list is a little smaller, at $13.50, if peanut butter and rice do not fit. The $20 shop is the one written out below, because it has enough foods to vary the week.

The seven days

The rice is 900 g, the oats are 1 kg, and the beans are one can. The scoop is yours. The table only says which foods share the plate, so dinner is never rice alone.

Seven days from the Canadian $20 list
DayMorningDinner
1OatsRice with beans, carrots, and spinach in oil, and an egg on the plate
2OatsRice with carrots and spinach in oil, and an egg

Six dinners include an egg, and night 3 does not. That matches the carton. Carrots, spinach, and oil show up every evening.
