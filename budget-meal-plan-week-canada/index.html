A budget meal plan for a week in Canada

Every amount on this page is for seven days, one week of groceries, not one day.

This week uses the Canadian $20 list. The foods add up to $19.81. Breakfast is bananas. Dinner is lentils or beans with cabbage, spinach, carrots, and tomato, cooked in oil.

Prices are the Canada average from Statistics Canada table 18-10-0245-01 for August 2026. The $15 list is a little smaller, at $13.74, if the carrots and the canned tomatoes do not fit. The $20 shop is the one written out below.

The seven days

The lentils are 900 g and the beans are one 540 ml can. Split them across the week. The table names what shares the plate, so dinner is never a single food.
