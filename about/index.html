About Budget Floor

A free grocery list for a tight week, by VELLURA Studios.

Each amount is seven days of groceries, one week, not one day. The foods are chosen to stay at or under that amount. A price is never cut to make the next food fit. If it would pass the amount, it stays off the list.

Where the prices come from

Canada prices are the Canada average from Statistics Canada table 18-10-0245-01 for August 2026. Adapted from Statistics Canada. This does not constitute an endorsement by Statistics Canada of this product.

United States prices are the U.S. city average from the Bureau of Labor Statistics average-price series for September 2026.

Mexico prices are Profeco observations for Mexico City in October 2026. A page about another Mexican city uses those same Mexico City observations. It is not a local price survey.

The site was reviewed in October 2026. Only one official month is stored for each country. Budget Floor does not show a change from one month to the next.

What the site stores is on the privacy page.

Food help

If the list is smaller than the week, these are public directories. Canada: 211.ca and Food Banks Canada. Quebec: 211 Québec and Banques alimentaires du Québec. United States: Feeding America. Mexico: Bancos de Alimentos de México and the map of food banks.
