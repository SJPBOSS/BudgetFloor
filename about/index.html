How the prices are calculated

Every amount on this page is for seven days, one week of groceries, not one day.

Budget Floor lists foods for seven days. The price beside each food is a published official price, or a whole multiple of that price. No price is lowered to fit a cap.

Canada

Canada prices are the Canada average from Statistics Canada table 18-10-0245-01 for August 2026. They are not a city flyer and not a store price. A partial package keeps the published rate. Vegetable oil is published as 3 litres at $9.82, and 500 ml is one sixth of that bottle, $1.64 to the cent. Half a kilogram of cabbage is half of $2.63, $1.32.

Adapted from Statistics Canada, Table 18-10-0245-01, August 2026. This does not constitute an endorsement by Statistics Canada of this product.

United States

United States prices are Bureau of Labor Statistics CPI average prices, U.S. city average, September 2026. The fact sheet is Average price data. A line that says three pounds is three times the published per-pound price. The figure is a U.S. city average. It is not the shelf price in one named city. These data are a U.S. government work.

Mexico

Mexico prices are Profeco Quién es Quién observations for Mexico City in October 2026, from qqp.profeco.gob.mx. The product name is the name on that observation, because the price belongs to that product. Other Mexican cities are not a separate survey. Budget Floor is not part of Profeco.

A change from last month

One official month is stored for each country. A percent change needs a second stored month. This site does not estimate one.

What these pages are

They are grocery lists and cooking steps. They are not medical advice, not a treatment, and not a halal or vegetarian certification. They do not name a grocery chain as a place to shop, do not use store logos, and do not carry affiliate or commission links. The lists stay free. Food-help links are public directories.

What stays on this phone

Language, country, amount, checklist marks, and large type stay in this browser. Budget Floor has no account and does not ask for an email. A service worker can save the site files on the device so a list you already opened can load without a connection. It does not contact any other site. Read aloud uses your browser’s speech. Budget Floor does not receive a recording.

The address /pedido/ is a separate sample order page. It is not a grocery budget.
