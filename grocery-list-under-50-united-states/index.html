A grocery list under $50 a week in the United States

Every amount on this page is for seven days, one week of groceries, not one day.

The $50 United States list is for seven days. The foods add up to $49.814, inside $50. The $40 list adds up to $39.486, also inside $50. Both use the U.S. city average for September 2026.

A pound of sugar is $1.019 and would pass $50, so sugar stays off the list. The price was not cut. This basket may not feed four people.
