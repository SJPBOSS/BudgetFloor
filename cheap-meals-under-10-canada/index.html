Cheap meals in Canada under a set amount

People search for cheap meals under $5 or under $10. In Budget Floor those amounts are whole grocery lists, not the price of one prepared plate at a counter. Here is what each cap buys, and the meals those foods become. Every price is a Canadian line from the tool.

Under $5

Two items. No Name rice at $2.75. Dried lentils at $2.25. Together $5.00.

900g Rice (No Name)$2.75
450g Dried Lentils$2.25

The meal is rice and lentils, cooked with plenty of water. That water line is the $5 instruction. There is no egg, vegetable, oil, or sauce on this list. The tool calls anything under $5 in 2026 emergency territory. If this is the cap, use 211 as well as the pot.

If this shop is more than you have, use a directory. 211.ca connects you to local help, and Food Banks Canada covers the country. In Quebec, 211 Québec and the Banques alimentaires du Québec do that job.

Under $25 and under $30

At $25 the list adds 900 g of pasta at $1.75, pasta sauce at $1.75, and 1 kg of flour at $1.50. The total is $25.00. The meal change is pasta with sauce, so dinner is not only rice. The flour is in the basket. The butter, sugar, and milk a baked recipe usually needs are not, so they are not part of a meal here.

At $30 the list adds a bag of onions at $2.50, salt and pepper at $1.50, and hot sauce at $1.00. The total is $30.00. The meal is the same pot, with onion in it and hot sauce at the table.

How to choose a cap

  • The smallest shop that still names a morning food and an evening food with some protein is the $10 list. Read Grocery budget for one person if that plate is yours alone.
  • A written week sits on Budget meal plan for a week.
  • More than one person means the eggs and the single can of beans run out first. Read Grocery budget for a family before you treat $10 as a household shop.
  • Every line, from $5 through $30, is on Cheap grocery list for Canada.
