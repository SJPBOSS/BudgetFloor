A grocery budget for a family in Canada

The largest Canadian list on Budget Floor is the $30 shop. The foods add up to $29.75. It is still a careful week, even for two people, and it will not cover a bigger household by itself. If several people are eating from this one basket, the food-help links below are part of the plan, not a last resort you have to earn.

The pot can be shared: lentil and carrot stew one night, rice and beans with spinach another, pasta with sauce and onion another. Oil is in the shop, so the vegetables are cooked, not just set beside dry rice. Prices are estimated, October 2026. No store page is on file, and bananas are not priced.

If this shop is more than you have, use a directory. 211.ca connects you to local help, and Food Banks Canada covers the country. In Quebec, 211 Québec and the Banques alimentaires du Québec do that job. In the United States, Feeding America finds a food bank.
