A cheap grocery list for the United States

Every amount on this page is for seven days, one week of groceries, not one day.

This is a one-week grocery list for the United States. The prices are the U.S. city average from the Bureau of Labor Statistics for September 2026. They are not the shelf price in New York, Houston, Los Angeles, Chicago, or any other named city.

The amounts on the list are $5, $10, $15, $20, $25, $30, $40, $50, and $100 for seven days. A pound that would pass the cap is left off. The price is not cut to fit. A pound of sugar is $1.019 and does not fit the $50 list or the $100 list.
